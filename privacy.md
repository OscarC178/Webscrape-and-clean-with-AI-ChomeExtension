# Privacy Policy for RAG Text Scraper

**Effective Date:** October 24, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how RAG Text Scraper handles your information.

## Information We Handle

RAG Text Scraper is designed to work locally on your browser. We do not collect or store any of your personal data.

* **API Keys:** If you use the "Clean with AI" feature, you must provide your own API key from a third-party AI provider (Google, OpenAI, or Anthropic). This key is stored exclusively on your local computer using the browser's storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). It is never sent to us or any other server, except directly to the AI provider you select to process your request. For your security, we strongly recommend using an API key with strict usage limits.
* **Scraped Web Content:** The extension processes the text content of webpages you choose to scrape. This content is handled locally and, if you enable the AI feature, is sent directly to your chosen AI provider for cleaning. This data is not seen, stored, or logged by the extension.

## Third-Party Services

When you use the AI cleaning feature, your text data is sent to the AI provider you have configured. We recommend you review the privacy policy of your chosen provider:

* Google: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* OpenAI: [https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)
* Anthropic: [https://www.anthropic.com/privacy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)
